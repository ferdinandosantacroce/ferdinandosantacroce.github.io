You can write to me at [ferdinando.santacroce@gmail.com](mailto:ferdinando.santacroce@gmail.com). I usually reply within a couple of working days.

If you are writing about a project, three lines are enough: what you are building, what the problem is, and what you need. If it is an invitation to speak, tell me where and when, and who the audience is.
